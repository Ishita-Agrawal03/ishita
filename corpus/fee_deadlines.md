## Late Payment Policy

Payments received after the due dates above are subject to a late fee calculated as **2% of the total outstanding fee amount for every week of delay**, compounding weekly, up to a maximum of six weeks, beyond which the matter is referred to the Finance Committee under Section 9.4.

## Refund Policy

- Withdrawal before the start of classes: 90% refund of tuition fee.
- Withdrawal within the first two weeks of classes: 50% refund of tuition fee.
- Withdrawal after two weeks of classes: no refund of tuition fee. Hostel fees are refunded on a pro-rated monthly basis regardless of the withdrawal date.
- Examination Fee, Late Registration Fee, and Convocation Fee are non-refundable under all circumstances.

## Payment Modes

Fees may be paid via the Institute's online payment portal (net banking, UPI, debit/credit card) or by demand draft submitted to the Accounts Office. Cash payments are not accepted for amounts exceeding ₹10,000.

## Installment Payment Option

Students facing temporary cash-flow difficulty, as distinct from the documented financial hardship covered under Section 9.4 of the Regulations, may apply to the Accounts Office for a two-installment payment plan for tuition fee only (not hostel, examination, or other fees). The application must be submitted before the original due date, and approval is at the discretion of the Accounts Officer. Under an approved installment plan, 60% of tuition is due on the original date and the remaining 40% is due six weeks later; late fee provisions apply independently to each installment if missed.

## Sibling and Alumni Discounts

The Institute does not offer sibling discounts, alumni discounts, or any other fee concession based on family or alumni relationship. All fee concessions are limited to the Scholarship categories described in Section 8 of the Regulations and the hardship-based waivers described in Section 9.4.

## Bank Details for Demand Draft

Demand drafts should be drawn in favour of "Institute of Applied Sciences and Technology," payable at the city in which the primary campus is located. The draft number and date must be submitted to the Accounts Office along with a copy of the fee challan within three working days of purchase to be considered as payment received on the purchase date rather than the submission date.
